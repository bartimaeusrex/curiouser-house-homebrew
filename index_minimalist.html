Welcome! Curiouser House Publishing is a one-man-band-side-hustlin'-independent-small-business-publishing person bringing you new editions of classic literature and books that deserve to be considered classics, however forgotten they may be at the moment. I strive also to include any appropriate supplementary materials such as essays or illustrations to enhance the text and bring context to the author and their story.

For now, most books are only available through Amazon, but I intend publishing them through Ingram so that they will be more widely available to bookstores, libraries, and also Bookshop.org. I have dreams of one day having the time and energy to create both softcover and hardcover editions and, my goodness, be able to commission new artwork and essays. As it is: all editorial research, graphic design, layout, marketing, etc, is all down to me. In an ideal world this passion project would be my full time gig and I would be able to release new editions regularly. Until then, I present our current selection:
