Oscar Wilde's only novel, the classic gothic tale of the Victorian fin-de-siecle. Published in serial form in 1890, and then as a novel a year later, it caused a sensation.

Included in this edition are contemporary reviews, critiques, and editorials, along with many of Oscar Wilde's personal replies, in a collection originally edited by Stuart Mason (Art and Morality: a Record of the Discussion Which Followed the Publication of Dorian Gray), and published in 1908. Such discourse provides a rich context for the society in which Wilde lived.
